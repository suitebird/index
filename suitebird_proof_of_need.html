Micro-occupancy

A word you'll hear again

Micro-occupancy is the practice of treating a vacant, move-in-ready unit as short-stay inventory. Instead of earning nothing while it waits for its permanent resident, the unit earns nightly revenue from guest stays, trial stays, and respite stays, and every guest who sleeps there is a warmer prospect than any web lead.

The key idea: micro-occupancy is additive. It does not replace the occupancy rate you already report. It stacks on top of it, because it earns nights from units that traditional occupancy counts as empty.

A quick example: a 120-unit community at 92% reports 92% to its board and its lender. Now three of its vacant units go guest-ready, each earning 12 nights a month at $150 a night. That is $5,400 in new monthly revenue, and those 36 earned nights, measured against the building's 3,600 available unit-nights, add a full point of occupancy. The community now operates at 93%: 92% traditional plus 1% micro-occupancy. Same building, same census, higher occupancy and NOI. And every one of those nights is a prospect sleeping in your building.

The proposed solution

What SuiteBird is

SuiteBird is a hospitality operating system for senior living. It gives a community everything needed to run guest stays as a real program rather than a favor: bookings, guest records, housekeeping turnover, and a pipeline that converts guests into residents. Six capabilities, one platform.

B

Booking engine

A public listing page for each suite with real availability, rates, and online booking. Friends and family stays, trial stays, and respite stays all run through the same calendar.

G

Guest CRM

Every guest becomes a record: who stayed, why, what they experienced, and where the follow-up stands. Nothing lives on a whiteboard or in a group text.

M

Make-ready workflow

A digital turnover system built for senior living housekeeping: departure inspection, full clean, and arrival staging as a step-by-step checklist on any device, with a 130-item unit onboarding list, linen and supply par levels, reorder triggers, and inspection sign-off before every arrival. A room attendant can turn a suite guest-ready without a supervisor in the room.

O

Micro-occupancy analytics

A second occupancy number that stacks on top of the one you already report: guest nights earned by vacant units, expressed as points added to your traditional occupancy rate, plus what each of those nights produced in revenue and pipeline.

P

Conversion pipeline

Trial-stay guests move from stay to follow-up to tour to application to move-in, with conversion tracked at every step. The stay becomes measurable census pipeline.

N

Flat-fee placement network

A member-aligned alternative to commission-based referral channels: a flat placement fee instead of a percentage of first month's rent, with the community keeping the guest relationship.

No furnished units? Suite Innovations, SuiteBird's furnishing arm, delivers complete guest-ready room packages, selected, delivered, and installed, so a community can activate its first suite in days rather than quarters.
